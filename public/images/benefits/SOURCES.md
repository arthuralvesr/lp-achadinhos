# Fotos da seção Vantagens

Fotografias de Vitaly Gariev, baixadas em 09/11/2026 do Unsplash. As páginas de origem identificam cada foto como gratuita sob a [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). São imagens ilustrativas, sem representar membros ou depoimentos do grupo.

- `garimpo.webp`: [Woman using phone while shopping with bags](https://unsplash.com/photos/woman-using-phone-while-shopping-with-bags-yd1N7a_328o).
- `whatsapp.webp`: [Woman using smartphone in cafe](https://unsplash.com/photos/woman-using-smartphone-in-cafe-lTYOzSMb1cE).
- `cupons.webp`: [A happy woman holding a credit card and smartphone](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-happy-woman-holding-a-credit-card-and-smartphone-xnnx06hdnYU).
- `livre.webp`: [Woman with coffee and phone on city street](https://unsplash.com/photos/woman-with-coffee-and-phone-on-city-street-Xl8LYNjr3bc).

Arquivos WebP locais com largura de 900 px. O enquadramento de cada card é feito via CSS.
